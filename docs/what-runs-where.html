How the numbers are made

Card memory = weights on the card + slots × (fixed state + KV per token × context) + compute buffers (which grow with context × slots) + 64 MiB for the CUDA context. RAM = the weights left on the host + the runtime's 322 MiB, against the machine's RAM less 2 GB for its system. Headroom is what the card keeps free; the hive's engine keeps 1 GB today. "Experts in RAM" leaves an MoE's attention on the card and moves its experts to RAM: decode slows to the speed of RAM, prefill slows more. A context past a model's trained length is never shown: the engine would allocate it, but no RoPE scaling is applied. Speeds are measured where named; slot counts past four are projected from the measured one- and four-slot laws.
